# Proposed next Clifford experiment

This is a proposed bounded experiment, not an executed CG-01 result or a replacement for the existing TH2 preregistration.

The target is an arithmetic-selected Clifford action on a specified module. The numerical D4 fingerprint supplies a useful candidate operator environment. It does not choose spacetime, a real structure, or a signature.

1. **Fix the module and real structure.** Specify whether the operators act complex-linearly on the active C8, on the full C13, or real-linearly on H1_R of dimension 26. These are different problems. State the conjugation, metric, and allowed adjoints before looking for a favorable signature.
2. **Resolve elementary obstructions first.** On odd-dimensional C13, two invertible complex matrices cannot anticommute: AB=-BA would imply det(AB)=(-1)^13 det(BA), a contradiction. This is exact and independent of the numerical D4 fingerprint. It does not prohibit real-linear operators on R26 or operators on an enlarged module. Separately, if the fixed-five decomposition is established exactly, the unital algebra generated by this Lie algebra acts scalarly on that subspace and cannot furnish a nondegenerate anticommuting pair there.
3. **Verify the active projector and its transport.** Seek exact reconstruction or certified error bounds for the decomposition. When using theta gradients, transport covectors and the dual metric from the period frame into the frame of the projector. A Euclidean norm in two unrelated coordinate systems is not a basis-independent comparison.
4. **Register an arithmetic construction of candidate generators.** Specify the source operators and coefficients independently of the desired anticommutator outcome. Arbitrary searches inside a copy of so(8) can recover standard Clifford constructions; that alone would not show that arithmetic selects them.
5. **Measure the full anticommutator, not just its trace.** For module dimension d, form eta_ab = tr({Gamma_a,Gamma_b})/(2d) and residual R_ab = {Gamma_a,Gamma_b}-2 eta_ab I. Verify real-valued eta under the chosen real structure, nondegeneracy, inertia, and a stated bound on every R_ab. Check algebra closure and representation dimension separately.
6. **Keep normalization separate from signature.** A positive systole or trace normalization scales eta but cannot change its inertia or turn a non-scalar residual into zero. If using the systole, identify the cusped hyperbolic metric and its curvature-radius units.
7. **Test the arithmetic compatibility claimed.** Use the registered Hecke/Atkin–Lehner transformations in consistent frames. Current numerical screening finds that W11, W13, and W143 do not normalize the STAR-fixed triangle algebra. Any required invariance needs a new construction or an explicitly narrower target.

TH2 can run as a complementary support experiment after the frame and error-bound issues are resolved. An active-support result would constrain the next construction, but would not by itself establish a triality label or a Clifford signature.
