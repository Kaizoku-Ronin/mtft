# Proposed v0.27.0 research notes

This wording describes the supplied research integration. It is a draft for
review after integration, not an announcement that 0.27.0 has shipped.

## Research instruments and reproducibility

- Collect 15 reproducible studies spanning exact finite Ising thermodynamics,
  fields/topology, boundary sensing, prime-family traces, irregular-prime
  arithmetic, Hecke splitting, finite covariance controls, and arithmetic
  operator compression. Preserve source identities, fixed protocols, data,
  proofs, independent controls, and negative results.
- Add a catalog, archive verification/extraction tools, and a portable
  electron–photon replay adapter for the explicit CC-21/CC-22 release contracts.
- Add an exact rational short-horizon certificate for the frozen T2 model:
  retaining four complement directions bounds relative Frobenius active
  propagation error by 0.601907% throughout 0<=t<=0.5. Independent rational
  verification accompanies the fixture. Physical time is not identified.

## Findings and interpretation

- Exact Hecke blocks remain invariant under tested arithmetic operators;
  the chosen active-eight split generally does not. Compressed operator
  noncommutativity can arise from omitted complementary coupling.
- W143 admits a special involution response formula. T2 has five-direction
  complementary memory and delayed coupling of initially dark active states.
- Lower-rank models fail the registered full spectral/long-horizon targets.
  Preserve all five complement directions for those targets. The new bound
  is limited to its declared short interval and matrix-error metric.
- Finite prime-family marks and irregular-prime censuses retain theorem/open
  status separately from exact membership. No new prime-gap or infinitude
  theorem is claimed.

## Compatibility

- Reuse the existing exact Ising DOS implementation as the public route;
  retain the independent study engine as a cross-check.
- Preserve both Higgs quartic conventions introduced in CC-22. The old local
  SM correction patch is historical and excluded from integration.
- Keep Gibbs boundary instruments separate from the existing canonical-ring
  `mtft.boundary` namespace. New public APIs require their own maintained
  contracts and installed-package validation.
