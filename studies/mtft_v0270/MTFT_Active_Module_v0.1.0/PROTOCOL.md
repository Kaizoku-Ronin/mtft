# Active module and arithmetic coupling — frozen protocol

Filed 8 September 2026 before computing the coupling observables in this study.
Source: the supplied MTFT 0.26.2 release, left unchanged. This is a numerical
module audit, not a Clifford construction, theta-gradient experiment, or proof
of a physical sector. It supplements the previous claim audit and TH2 protocol.

## Objects and inputs

Use the three primitive STAR-fixed channels from
`liealg.x0143_fixed_channels`, with their existing triangle choices (1,11,12).
Do not optimize those choices. Recover their common kernel directly by stacked
SVD, and compare with the numerical Lie-closure kernel and an independent
Hermitian-Gram route. Work in the returned Hodge-orthonormal adapted real frame;
the active complex projector becomes a rank-16 real projector if its complex
rank is eight. The complementary real rank is ten. Never call these eight and
five real dimensions.

Baseline period-input precision/frame seed: (50,143). Repeat at (50,2026),
(50,31415), and (80,143). These are NumPy double-precision matrix calculations;
raising period-input precision does not raise matrix arithmetic precision.

Primary operators: T2,T3,T5,T7,W11,W13,W143,STAR. Replication operators: T17,T19.
All matrices must be transported from their documented source frame into the
same adapted frame. Verify complex linearity (or STAR antilinearity), relevant
metric-adjoint identities, and arithmetic commutation identities as convention
checks. Do not confuse subspace preservation with normalization of the D4 algebra.

## Measurements and numerical gates

Record complete singular spectra. For normalized singular values, <=1e-9 is
the small band and >=1e-7 the large band. Values strictly between the bands
make a rank decision ambiguous. Record projector idempotence, orthogonality,
primitive annihilation, and cross-route/cross-frame differences.

Let P be the active orthogonal projector and Q=I-P in the adapted real frame.
For each arithmetic operator A, record all four Frobenius block norms:
PAP, PAQ, QAP, QAQ. Record their squared fractions of ||A||_F^2; these are
operator-energy bookkeeping, not transition probabilities. Also record
||QAP||_F/||AP||_F and ||PAQ||_F/||AQ||_F (flag zero denominators), and the
normalization-independent full commutator residual ||AP-PA||_F/||A||_F.

For leakage normalized by ||A||_F: <=1e-8 is numerically unresolved/consistent
with preservation; >=1e-6 is resolved mixing; intermediate values are ambiguous.
These thresholds are diagnostic gates, not certified roundoff bounds. Report
stability under the registered frame and input-precision changes; do not rename
empirical stability as a proof or interval enclosure.

Compute the smallest numerically invariant real subspace generated by the
active space under (a) T2 alone, (b) the primary good Hecke operators, (c) the
Atkin–Lehner operators, and (d) the entire primary set. Use repeated span
augmentation, normalize each nonzero input operator before augmentation, use
the same singular-value bands, and record every growth step and terminal
leakage. Include J as a closure check on each final span, rather than silently
adding it as an arithmetic generator. Check the replication operators on the
final primary hull. Full rank 26 means the hull fills H1_R; it is not a
claim of irreducibility of the entire Hecke representation.

## Real structure and controls

Check STAR^2=I, STAR J=-J STAR, and metric compatibility. Measure its active
and fixed restrictions. Any invariance forced by STAR-fixed primitive channels
is a structural consistency check, not independent discovery.

Compare the direct primitive common kernel with the Lie-closure kernel and
with a separate reconstruction. Coordinate-seed changes are covariance checks,
not statistically independent samples. Identity/full-space controls must have
zero leakage. No random search for favorable primes, triangles, dimensions,
signatures, or cutoffs is permitted in this run.

## Interpretation and reporting

Preservation permits a restriction of the registered arithmetic action to the
active sector; it does not select a Clifford signature. Resolved mixing rules
out isolation under the responsible operators but leaves coupling or enlarged
module constructions open. An unstable projector blocks geometric conclusions.
Persist scripts, source hashes, complete results, a coupling figure, and a
written assessment. Any follow-up prompted by the measurements must be labeled
exploratory and appended with its rationale rather than changing this protocol.

## Amendment A — channel types, before coupling results

Source inspection distinguishes the three returned matrices B_i, which encode
antilinear maps z -> B_i conjugate(z), from the three complex-linear Lie seeds
C_ij = B_i conjugate(B_j) - B_j conjugate(B_i) used by `d4_report`.
The primary direct reconstruction of the D4 active projector will use the
common kernel of these three C_ij. The independent check will also report
the common kernel of the original antilinear maps with the required conjugation.
No equality of these two kernels is assumed. This resolves the ambiguous phrase
"three primitive channels" above before any coupling observables were read.
