# HOPF-02: arithmetic Hopf geometry and the meaning of level 143

Date: 2026-09-17. Additive investigation; no package or previous report is changed.

1. Reconstruct the rational cuspidal T2 operator using the supplied 0.31.4
   Manin-symbol implementation. Restrict to its quartic block. Check the
   minimal polynomial, real-root count, and centralizer dimension. Test whether
   a quaternionic action commuting with T2 is possible on the original block;
   construct an explicit doubled algebraic module as a positive control.
2. Verify the Q8 matrices and projective transformations of the previously
   established half-form pencil (1, sqrt(13)u). Recompute the eta-quotient cusp
   orders. Determine the degree of the resulting map to CP1 and the reduction
   of the quaternionic Hopf connection along CP1 inside HP1.
3. Compute the Hopf curvature symbolically in a four-real-coordinate chart,
   check anti-self-duality in the specified orientation, and integrate its
   second Chern density. Compare with the induced two-dimensional curvature.
4. Enumerate the CRT factorization of P1(Z/143), its S,T permutations and cusp
   cycles. Check the three modular-curve genera, map degrees, sphere product,
   join and tensor dimensions, and the generalized quaternionic Hopf dimensions.

Scope: exact algebra and topology. A metric compatible with a companion
operator is an algebraic control, not the measured Petersson metric. The
earlier exact eta transformation identities are inputs, not re-proved here.
No claimed spacetime dynamics, observed-particle fit, or newly derived parent
gauge theory follows from these tests.
