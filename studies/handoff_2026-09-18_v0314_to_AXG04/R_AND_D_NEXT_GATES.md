# Next research gates

These are proposed investigations, not completed results or promises that a viable solution exists. Freeze each candidate's assumptions before testing it. Record both a reproducible failure witness and a successful construction when available.

## Gate 1 — Define C3X's global tensor theory

**Question:** can the local integral factorization be promoted to a globally defined quantum cancellation sector for the actual direct-product gauge group?

**Inputs:** the complete C3X spectrum; Ω; characteristic/source vectors; spin structure; integral X4/Y4; axion periods; allowed gauge bundles; tensor/string source and patching data.

**Required output:** a differential-cohomology/global action construction or a precise obstruction, including large gauge transformations and the class of field configurations on which it is defined. Explain how dH=X4 is realized when X4 has nonzero integral class. Recheck what bordism category applies after specifying the tensor data.

**Pass:** the exponentiated action and anomaly cancellation are well defined in the declared category. **Fail/open:** a formal local descent, integral polynomial or ordinary Ω7Spin(BG)=0 is the only evidence. The existing bordism calculation remains a useful necessary ingredient, not the missing construction.

## Gate 2 — Change the AdS/scale-separation result with a specified action

**Question:** what justified addition to the action or background can evade the current unwarped product relation λ4=−R⁻²−2ρF/M?

Choose one mechanism (for example a specified source, extra dynamical field or controlled warped ansatz) and derive its stress tensor and equations. Do not introduce an unconstrained fitted radion potential after seeing the desired minimum.

**Required output:** full background equations, flux quantization, all field stationarity conditions, kinetic signs and curvature scales. A solution must address external curvature and scale separation, not only a positive radion Hessian.

**Pass:** a solution in a stated regime of validity that changes the adverse relation and survives the declared stability tests. **Fail:** no stationary solution, hidden negative kinetic energy, an arbitrary scale input presented as a prediction, or a solution whose neglected terms are not controlled. An AdS solution may still be a useful result, labeled accordingly.

## Gate 3 — Account for the remaining low-energy fields

**Question:** what happens to the 72 real shape moduli, the two-form descendants, the extra vector/remnant, and any new matter required by the global completion?

**Required output:** a complete zero/light-mode inventory with kinetic operators, gaugings, masses, anomaly contributions and couplings. Gauge-fix before interpreting the physical Hessian. Test mixing with the radion and Higgs, and use the appropriate AdS stability criterion where applicable.

**Pass:** the retained effective theory has a justified truncation or an explicit treatment of the remaining fields. **Fail/open:** they disappear from the model merely because they were absent from a small scalar truncation.

## Gate 4 — Decide which family mechanism is being pursued

Two routes remain conceptually distinct:

| Route | Benefit | Unresolved requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Continue C3X | Explicit local action, pure unit-flux mode and integral factorization | Three parent copies and four flavor matrices are inputs; derive or justify their origin |
| Recover one parent × three internal modes | Keeps the original arithmetic-family motivation | Supply a new consistent charged spectrum/global form/operator that escapes the rejected index-three controls |

**Required output:** actual h0/h1 or kernel dimensions for the specified fields, allowed parent Yukawas, canonical overlap tensors and an independently specified vacuum-selection mechanism. Do not combine C3X anomaly data with M1 flavor tensors without proving the bridge.

**Pass:** the same parent/action supplies the spectrum, interactions and vacuum used to compute flavor. **Fail/open:** the target masses determine arbitrary input matrices or Higgs directions and are then described as predictions.

## Gate 5 — Fix one physical scale

**Question:** is at least one mass/coupling in physical units determined by the model, or explicitly calibrated with a declared input while yielding another testable output?

**Required output:** dimensional analysis of every coupling; Einstein-frame normalization; radius determination; KK and EFT cutoff hierarchy; treatment of renormalization if comparing different scales. Separate derived quantities, calibrated inputs and free parameters.

**Pass:** a falsifiable relation or number whose inputs and uncertainty are stated. **Fail/open:** a dimensionless arithmetic value is labeled GeV without a dynamical/calibration map. M_P²=M6⁴A is a reduction relation, not a scale-selection mechanism.

## Alternative 7D branch — only after changing its mode problem

If continuing the seven-dimensional route, first specify the boundary/defect/singular data or other mechanism that changes the smooth closed local-system calculation. Derive the new operator, domain, index and anomaly/inflow conditions before counting families. The old bulk cohomology result remains a regression test in the smooth source-free limit. Merely changing radii or base Wilson lines cannot cure its nontrivial-fiber acyclicity.

## How to close a gate

Save the preregistered assumptions, exact inputs and conventions; implementation; raw result ledger; independent or negative controls; interpretation; and source hashes. A bounded no-go result is a completed investigation if its assumptions and witness are explicit. No failed branch needs to be relabeled successful to justify building its tools.
