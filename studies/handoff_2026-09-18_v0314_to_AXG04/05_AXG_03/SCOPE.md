# AXG-03 scope

Date: 2026-09-17. Follow-up to AXG-02. This scope records an investigation,
not a blind preregistration of previously unexamined hypotheses.

1. Audit the remaining anomaly polynomial of the explicit nonsupersymmetric
   6D cd-chirality-flipped matter assignment. Distinguish formal factorization,
   tensor multiplicity/signature, chiral tensor anomalies, and kinetic positivity.
2. Enforce integral tensor transgression and periodic axion charges for the
   fixed internal flux m=(0,-3,3,3,0). Do not rescale axion periods silently.
3. Audit the discrete remnant, matter operators and discrete fermion anomaly of
   the explicit integral control Khat=(k2-k1;3k1). This is not assumed to be a
   complete quantum-consistent gauge theory.
4. Test minimally coupled elementary Higgs scalars and their possible local
   six-dimensional Yukawa interactions. Do not transfer a twisted Dirac,
   Dolbeault, or off-diagonal-vector zero-mode claim to an ordinary scalar
   Laplacian without deriving the physical operator.

No package or earlier artifact is changed. All necessary conditions are scoped
to their assumptions. A restriction or counterexample does not exclude all
possible MTFT parents.
