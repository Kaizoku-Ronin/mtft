# AXG-02 scope

Date: 2026-09-17. Follow-up to AXG-01 using the supplied MTFT 0.31.4 M1 data.

This records the investigated assumptions, not a blind preregistration.

1. Test the literal non-supersymmetric 6D matter lift: one complex Weyl fermion
   of a common chirality per unordered bifundamental pair, with the package's
   stack ranks and fluxes. Compute [Ahat ch]_8 and its pushforward to 4D.
2. Distinguish ordinary two-form Green–Schwarz factorization from generalized
   zero-form cancellation. Test alternative chirality in zero-index sectors
   without claiming unchanged massive or full zero-mode spectra.
3. Test whether native smooth, fixed-modulus 6D Stückelberg zero-forms with
   AXG-01's charge matrix can exist on the nontrivial internal flux background.
   Exclude axion defects and radial zeros only for this bounded test.
4. Derive the potential scaling from an unwarped 6D Einstein–Yang–Mills plus
   neutral-scalar ansatz. Classify a single positive exponential flux energy
   and a single bulk exponential of either sign. The action and exponents
   are conditional controls, not derived MTFT interactions.

No new parent is declared physically consistent merely because a polynomial
factorizes. Complete anomaly cancellation, kinetic positivity, quantization,
scalar interactions and vacuum equations remain separate gates.
