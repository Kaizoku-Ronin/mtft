## Small presentation erratum discovered while assembling this handoff

**HANDOFF-TYPO-01 — SC7-01 §8 local metric.** The preserved original report omits a plus sign between the two summands in one displayed coordinate formula. Read that display as

\[
ds_P^2=R^2\frac{dx^2+dy^2}{y^2}
\; + \; r^2\left(d\theta+\frac12\frac{dx}{y}\right)^2.
\]

The preceding abstract formula `ds_P²=ds_X²+r²Theta²`, the script's metric and the quoted curvature already use the sum. No archived source or historical numerical result was changed for this typographic note.

## Rule for supersession

Append an entry giving the prior claim, the new claim, its assumptions and the decisive witness. Retain old artifacts and their hashes. A new model can resolve a prior model's obstruction by changing its assumptions; this does not make the earlier obstruction erroneous. Conversely, a correction to a transformation law or contraction must not be disguised as a new optional model.
