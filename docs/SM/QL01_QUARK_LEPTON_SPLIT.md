# QL-01 — the quark–lepton split (2026-10-06)

Module `mtft.matter.quark_lepton_split`; tests `tests/test_ql01_quark_lepton_split.py`.

1. **The b–tau bound (EXACT).** Kinetic dressing only lowers a Yukawa (Z <= 1). With tree-level b–tau equality and d^c undressed (CD-1), explaining
   (m_mu/m_tau)/(m_s/m_b) = 3.09 by dressing forces y_tau/y_b = Z_3^e <= 0.32 at the matching scale. GC-01's running gives 1.40 (8.9e11 GeV) to 1.53 (2.1e14):
   a factor >= 4.4 (>= 3.1 even with b–tau unification). CD-4 retracted (CC-52); DP-3, DP-4, GT-4 caveat.
2. **The targets (NUMERICAL).** f_3 = 1.40–1.53, f_2 = 4.33–4.72, f_1 = 0.42–0.45 at matching; relative to the third family (running-independent) 3.09 and 0.30 —
   the Georgi–Jarlskog pattern (3, 1/3, 1) within 3% and 10%.
3. **What the vertex must supply (STRUCTURAL).** The condensate in the nu^c direction is the only SU(4)-breaking at the down-sector vertex and acts on the lepton
   component alone: f_j = 1 + kappa_j with kappa = (-0.58, +3.3, +0.40) at 8.9e11 GeV. The two families at P1 need opposite signs (odd under the jet parity, the
   gamma-flux's role in DD-01); the third a mild positive one. Only a condensate factor inside the Yukawa amplitude (XQ-01, YA-01) can do this.
4. **The Wilson-line split (ESTIMATE, caveat).** In the massless-W_R-gaugino limit it hits z_e = 2.7–3.3 at r ~ 100, but every lepton weight vanishes there.

Open: the condensate insertion's sign and size at the x-jet, w-jet and constant jet; whether f_1 f_2 = 1.9 is reproduced.
