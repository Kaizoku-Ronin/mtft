# CC-36 — tree-level Yukawas of exceptional-jet families vanish (2026-10-02)

**Retracted.** Statements that assumed nonzero tree-level Yukawa couplings between jet-localised families under the Scherk–Schwarz twist:
- AL-01: "tree-level Yukawas are diagonal in the cusp basis; CKM = 1 at tree level; the diagonal entries are set by the Higgs section's values at the cusps."
  In the jet-only background the tree-level Yukawas are zero, diagonal and off-diagonal alike.
- PS-01 item 6 and NU-01 item 5 used tree-level Y_d = Y_e and Y_u = Y_nu. The SU(4) relations still hold for any SU(4)-symmetric mechanism that generates the
  couplings, in particular the matter-untwisted completion of YK-01 (its Wilson line is the same on colour and A1), so the b–tau scale and the seesaw requirements
  stand; their tree-level origin in the jet-only backgrounds does not.

**Reason (EXACT).** Two jet classes have Dolbeault representatives (dbar chi) h_1 / sigma^m and (dbar chi) h_2 / sigma^n with one cutoff chi; their wedge vanishes
pointwise. The zero-mode Yukawa is a cup product, so it vanishes for every pair of jet fermions, at one point or at different points.

**Unaffected.** Indices, D-flatness, anomaly tables, the one-loop adjoint masses, the localisation geometry of NU-02, the Atkin–Lehner obstruction of AL-01.
