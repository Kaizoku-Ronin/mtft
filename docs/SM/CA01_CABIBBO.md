# CA-01 — the Cabibbo angle and two measurements of the curve's size (2026-10-05)

Module `mtft.matter.cabibbo`; tests `tests/test_ca01_cabibbo.py`.

1. **The Cabibbo angle measures r (ESTIMATE).** EPS-01's gamma-tilt, eps ~ (2/3 ... 2 pi/3)|c| 3.216/sqrt(r) with r = A_X/A_E, is DD-3's parity breaking. V_us = 0.225 needs
   r = 91-896 (gamma-charge 1) or 363-3585 (charge 2).
2. **Two measurements disagree (STRUCTURAL).** V_cb with the top over a 2-torsion point needs r = 18.6-27.8. The ranges miss by a factor 3.3; EPS-01's down-sector tilt (230-2300)
   and NU-02's neutrino windows (96-280, 380-1080) side with the larger r. The 2-torsion home is the odd one out.
3. **A post-hoc candidate (MODEL).** The Fricke fixed point i/sqrt(143) — 0.107 from the cusp, fixed by W_143, torus point O fixed by the involution — gives V_cb = 0.041 at
   r = 79-105, overlapping the Cabibbo window's lower edge and NU-02's second-family window. Chosen after seeing the tension: a hypothesis for a third observable.
4. **V_ub (STRUCTURAL).** The data's c = 0.40 sits between the down-sector texture value (about 1) and the up-sector GST value (0.20).
5. **Vector-like pairs (ESTIMATE).** Charge 1 gives 5 pairs per block; TH-01 then puts the right-handed pairs near 3 TeV — within collider reach.

Open: the overlap coefficients behind the (2/3 ... 2 pi/3) band; a third observable at the Fricke point.
