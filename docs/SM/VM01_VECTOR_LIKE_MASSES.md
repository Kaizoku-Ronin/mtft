# VM-01 — what gives the vector-like pairs their masses (2026-10-05)

Module `mtft.matter.vector_like_masses`; tests `tests/test_vm01_vector_like_masses.py`.

1. **Not the condensate (EXACT).** An SU(4) invariant of fundamentals needs (#4 - #4bar) = 0 mod 4. Sigma psi psi' is forbidden; Sigma Sigma'-bar psi psi' is allowed but needs the
   conjugate condensate, absent in PB-01's one-orientation vacuum; Sigma^4 vanishes for a rank-one condensate. No holomorphic mass at any order.
2. **Nothing else at tree level (EXACT).** psi psi' is gauge-invariant; in Tr Phi [A, A] it needs adjoint Phi-type vevs, and every adjoint boson has a positive one-loop mass.
   Torus Wilson lines only move the gamma-localised pairs. All vector-like pairs are massless at tree level; TH-3 becomes a caveat.
3. **Consequences (STRUCTURAL).** TH-01's reconciliation needs a mass source not yet in the model; massless coloured vector-like states are excluded anyway, so the
   gamma-charged-matter design needs one, and it decides the left–right asymmetry.
4. **Candidate (ESTIMATE).** A conjugate condensate gives m ~ v_R^2/M_* on both sides (Clebsch channels 1 and 15). m_R ~ M_PS/5.6 needs M_* ~ 5.6 M_PS: the cutoff or
   Kaluza–Klein scale within a factor ~6 of the Pati–Salam scale.

Open: a second D-flat orientation; one-loop masses with a chirality source; the Clebsch asymmetry.
