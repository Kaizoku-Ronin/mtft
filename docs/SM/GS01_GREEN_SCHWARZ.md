# GS-01 — Green–Schwarz relaxation, the slepton direction, the vector-like symmetry (2026-10-05)

Module `mtft.matter.green_schwarz`; tests `tests/test_gs01_green_schwarz.py`.

1. **Relaxation undoes the condensate (EXACT).** The stack U(1)s are anomalous and their FIs are Kaehler data. With dynamical moduli, V = (1/2)[(xi - v^2)^2 + (5/8) v^4] is
   minimised at v = 0, xi = 0. The FI-driven condensate exists only if the moduli are stabilised at nonzero FI by something outside the D-terms; PS1-3 becomes a caveat.
2. **The slepton direction (EXACT).** The SU(4) D-term -v^2 (T^A)_44 cannot relax: +(1/8) g^2 v^2 for coloured (4,2,1) scalars, -(3/8) g^2 v^2 in the lepton direction. Only a
   fixed-moduli U(1) FI on the Q block lifts the slepton doublet; relaxed, the electroweak group breaks at the Pati–Salam scale.
3. **What protects the vector-like pairs (STRUCTURAL).** U(1)_V with Phi -2, A-type +1 leaves every zero-mode cubic and D-term invariant and forbids A A'. The KK pairing Phi_n A_n
   breaks it, so radiative masses are KK-mediated: m ~ (g^2 C/16 pi^2) M_SS F(M_SS/M_KK), with split partners suppressing the right-handed side further.

Open: what stabilises the Kaehler moduli at nonzero FI; the tower's loop function F.
