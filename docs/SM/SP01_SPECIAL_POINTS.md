# SP-01 — where the top can live (2026-10-05)

Module `mtft.matter.special_points`; tests `tests/test_sp01_special_points.py`.

1. **Distances (NUMERICAL).** Canonical (Arakelov) metric of area 1, from the Bergman density of the thirteen differentials (cusp density 0.02434 reproduces EPS-01). Path
   lengths from the blown-up cusp: 0.178 to the closest points over tau/2 and (1+tau)/2, 0.189 over the real 2-torsion point, 0.107 to i/sqrt(143), 0.214 to the opposite
   cusp. The region above height 0.0335 holds 15 per cent of the area.
2. **The curve's size (ESTIMATE).** V_cb = 0.041 with TA-01's (r/d)^3 tail fixes A_X ~ 18 A_E (top blow-up A_E/3) to 34-36 A_E (0.55 A_E) for a top over a 2-torsion point
   on the graph of phi, and 19-32 A_E for a top at another cusp. With no torus flux, D-flatness leaves A_X free, so the mixing measures it.
3. **CC-37 revisited (STRUCTURAL; CC-47).** The other cusps are only slightly farther than the 2-torsion points; at A_X ~ 18 A_E their mixing is 0.034-0.046, not <= 1/143.
   CK-01's bound used the hyperbolic distance log 143 with exponential decay; at the smooth blown-up cusp tails fall as powers. CK1-1 and CC-37 become caveats.
4. **A coincidence worth keeping.** The points over E's 2-torsion are where gamma-charged fermions localise (NU-01); the alternative homes are the Atkin–Lehner images of the
   jets' cusp.

Open: which home the dynamics picks; an independent handle on A_X (gauge couplings); the tail law beyond leading scaling.
