# VS-01 — the condensate's feedback on the (4,2,1) scalars (2026-10-05)

Module `mtft.matter.vacuum_stability`; tests `tests/test_vs01_vacuum_stability.py`.

1. **Feedback (EXACT, tree level).** Sigma in the 4bar has U(1)_4 charge -1 (D_4 = xi_4 - v^2) and SU(4) D-term -v^2 (T^A)_44. A (4,2,1) scalar in direction b has
   m^2 = g^2[xi_4L - v^2] + Sum_A D^A (T^A)_bb with Sum_A (T^A)_44 (T^A)_bb = (delta_b4 - 1/4)/2: squark doublets g^2[xi_4L - (7/8) v^2], slepton doublet
   g^2[xi_4L - (11/8) v^2], v^2 = (8/13) xi_R4.
2. **Consequence (EXACT).** A D-flat Q block (TP-4's relation, FS-1's criterion) is tachyonic once the condensate forms: colour and the electroweak group would break at
   the Pati–Salam scale. Stability needs xi_4L >= (11/13) xi_R4 (CC-49).
3. **Ordering (STRUCTURAL).** With exceptional terms entering both FIs alike, (a_Q, a_u) = (11, 13) is unstable for any E > 0 and (13, 11) is stable: a_Q > a_u.
4. **Asymmetry (ESTIMATE).** Left-handed partners near the edge (light), right-handed at the Pati–Salam scale (CO-3): m_R/m_L ~ (M_SS/M_PS)^2; TH-01's factor five needs
   M_SS ~ M_PS/2, and then the left-handed vector-like pairs, at (alpha C/4 pi) M_SS, are the lightest new states — near the TeV scale.

Open: the loop function; Green–Schwarz relaxation of the anomalous U(1)s; the two-U(1) coefficients.
