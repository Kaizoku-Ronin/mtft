# SM-01 campaign — steps 2–5 on X0(143) (preregistered 2026-09-13)

Parent (declared): a 6D gauge theory with group U(3)_c x U(2)_L x U(1)_a x U(1)_b (optionally x U(1)_d), one
line-bundle flux L_x per stack (SU(3), SU(2) parts trivial so colour and weak isospin survive), fermions in all
bifundamentals (x, y-bar) with internal bundle S0 (x) L_x (x) L_y^-1, S0 the even arithmetic spin structure.
Net left-handed multiplicity of (x, y-bar) = deg L_x − deg L_y (index theorem; negative = conjugate).
Hypercharge Y = sum_x y_x Q_x with y_x in (1/6) Z.

Step 2 gate: exhaustive search over degree differences in [−8, 8] and hypercharge vectors in [−1, 1] for
chiral content exactly 3 x {Q(3,2,1/6), u^c(3bar,1,−2/3), d^c(3bar,1,1/3), L(1,2,−1/2), e^c(1,1,1)}, with
nu^c(1,1,0) and vector-like pairs permitted, no other chiral exotics.  Outcomes: the list of solutions or the
exact negative.  Each solution then gets: U(1)_x anomaly coefficients (GS-cancellable is a parent assumption;
hypercharge must be anomaly-free — automatic for exact SM content), W13-realisability of each flux, and
Brill–Noether purity of each bifundamental with S0.
Step 3: anomaly ledger for the chosen solution.  Step 4: Higgs doublets from index-0 sectors (h^0 = h^1),
electroweak breaking as recombination, post-Higgs spectrum by cohomology.  Step 5: Riemann–Roch section
bases for the flux bundles (weight-2 forms over a W13-even form) and Yukawa cup products; constructive E2 of
the purity theorem.  No scale, coupling value, or fit is claimed anywhere.
